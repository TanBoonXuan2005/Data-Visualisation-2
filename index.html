The places
we keep.

A visual record of Australian heritage — where it lies, what it means and why its future matters.

Australia's heritage spans natural landscapes, historic places and Indigenous culture. This record draws on 126 National Heritage places and 21 Australian World Heritage properties to explore four connected questions.

READER’S NOTE

When a World Heritage property is under threat, UNESCO asks the government for a State of Conservation report. Since 1983 it has reviewed 14 of Australia's 21 properties in 88 reports. All 14 are natural or mixed properties; none of the five purely cultural properties appears in this report dataset.
